## Why Fabrica

> "Fourteen tabs. Three broken contexts. It's 11 PM. Direct the crew instead."

Solo founders and lean teams don't need a fourth chatbot — they need a crew they
can direct. Fabrica runs parallel **Developer, Researcher, Marketer, and Business
Analyst** agents, each in its own isolated git worktree, supervised from a single
command center.

### The crew
| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| 👨‍💻 Developer | Fullstack builds & patches in isolated worktrees, clean diffs |
| 🔎 Researcher | Market & source intelligence, verified synthesis |
| 📣 Marketer | Positioning, outreach & launch angles |
| 📈 Analyst | Unit economics & financial guardrails |

### The guardrails
- **Hard budget auto-stops** — execution halts the instant spend hits the cap
- **1-tap approval gates** — payments, deploys & campaigns wait for your sign-off
- **BYOK client-side vault** — AES-256 GCM, zero cloud key storage
- **24/7 daemons** — with mobile oversight from your phone
